Privacy Policy

Effective October 24, 2026. Short version: BrowserSearch doesn't collect, sell or share any data about you.

What BrowserSearch reads

When you open the search panel, BrowserSearch reads the titles and URLs of tabs in browsers that are already open, so it can search them. This happens on your Mac, and the list is kept in memory only until it's replaced by the next search. It is never written to disk.

What BrowserSearch stores

Only your settings: the search shortcut. Launch at login is managed by macOS.

Network access

None. BrowserSearch makes no network connections.

What BrowserSearch does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • No browsing history, page contents, cookies or passwords are read.
  • No keystroke logging. BrowserSearch is only told when your search shortcut is pressed.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue. Please don't include personal information; issues are public.
