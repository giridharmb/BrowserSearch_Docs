Find any tab in a keystroke.

BrowserSearch lives in your menu bar. Press ⌥Space, type a few letters, and jump straight to the matching tab in Chrome, Safari or Firefox, whichever window it's in.

macOS 14 Sonoma or later · Nothing leaves your Mac

Built for people with too many tabs

One shortcut, every browser, no mouse.

⌨️

Global hotkey

Works from any app. ⌥Space by default, or record your own in Settings.

🔎

Fuzzy search

Matches titles and URLs. gh pull finds “Pull requests · GitHub”; skipped letters still match.

🧭

Every browser at once

Google Chrome, Safari and Firefox, side by side in one list with each browser's icon.

🎯

Straight to the tab

Raises the right window and selects the tab, even when you have dozens of windows open.

⚡️

Instant

The panel opens with your last list immediately, then refreshes. Reading a hundred tabs takes a fraction of a second.

💤

Never launches browsers

Only browsers that are already open are searched. If only Chrome is running, only Chrome is searched.

🚀

Launch at login

Turn it on once in Settings and the hotkey is always ready.

🔒

Private

Tab titles and URLs are read on your Mac, used for the search, and never stored or sent anywhere.
